Handmade African geometric mirrors. Crafted in Nairobi.

Reclaimed timber, cut into triangles by hand and set into frames that turn a wall into a piece of architecture.

See the mirrors

Made slowly, from wood that already had a life.

Every Konge mirror starts as reclaimed timber. We cut each triangle by hand, fit it against the next, and finish the grain so the pattern reads from across a room. The shapes come from African geometric art: repeated, rhythmic, and built to last.
